FAQ

Your questions, answered.

How accurate is the translation?
Menufy uses the latest AI to deliver high-accuracy translations across printed menus, handwritten menus, chalkboards, and digital tablets. Beyond just dish names, it also explains ingredients and cooking methods so you know exactly what you're ordering.
Does it work offline?
An internet connection is required for AI translation. Wi-Fi or cellular data both work. The last exchange rate is kept on the device, so history conversions still show offline.
Is my privacy protected?
Photos you capture are used only for translation and are never stored on our servers. Translation history is saved on your device. We never sell or share your data with third parties.
Is this a subscription?
No — Menufy uses one-time purchases only. Credits you buy never expire, and there are no recurring charges or auto-renewals.
Can I use my credits on multiple devices?
Yes. Purchased credits sync automatically — via iCloud on iOS, and via your Google account on Android — so they are shared across all devices signed in with the same account and survive reinstalls.
Can I try it for free?
Yes — download the app and your first 5 translations are free. If you like it, choose from 25, 150, or 400 credit packs to match your travel style.
Can I use it with food allergies?
Yes. Register your dietary needs (vegetarian, vegan, halal, and common allergies — 11 options) and Menufy flags dishes that may conflict, and states your needs on the order card. Note: flags are AI-generated, so if you have a severe allergy, always confirm with the restaurant as well.
Can I order without speaking the language?
Yes. Select the dishes you want and Menufy shows a full-screen order card in the menu's own language — a polite request phrase, your allergy statements and your dishes. Just show your phone to the staff. A speaker button pronounces each line.
How does the bill check work?
Photograph the receipt after the meal. Every line is checked against your order and the menu prices: unordered items, prices above the menu, and the service charge and tax breakdown are flagged, with questions to ask the staff in the local language. One check uses one scan credit.
Do I have to pick the menu language and currency?
No. By default both are detected from the photo. If you want to pin them, choose a language or currency in settings and auto-detect turns off.
How is this different from Google Translate?
Menufy is built for menus. Instead of swapping words, it understands each dish and organizes descriptions, prices and warnings on one screen. Then it goes further: picks, an order card in the local language, and a bill check.
